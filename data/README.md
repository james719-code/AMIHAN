# FieldWise data package

This package contains the data utilities and the original exports needed to regenerate FieldWise’s historical climate data. Keep larger local downloads in `raw/` and other generated files in `processed/`.

## Consolidated CSV

[fieldwise.csv](processed/fieldwise.csv) is the combined analysis table. It contains one row per dataset, location, and period, with separate columns for each measurement. The `dataset`, `period`, and `time_standard` columns identify the type of observation. Blank cells indicate missing or inapplicable values. Zero remains a valid reading.

| Dataset | Rows | What a row represents |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `nasa-power-municipal` | 41,724 | One UTC day in 2024 at a town or city marker, with 13 measures and the four source grid cell IDs. |
| `nasa-power-historical` | 22,828 | One Local Solar Time day, month, or year from the original 2015–2024 point exports. Annual rainfall retains the reported mean daily rate. |
| `nasa-imerg-giovanni` | 120 | One month for the study-area average. The CSV includes the original mm/hour rate and the converted monthly depth. |
| `pagasa-station-normals` | 48 | One calendar month in the 1991–2020 station baseline, with the month and baseline years stored explicitly. |
| `bswm-soilgrids` | 114 | Soil information at one mapped town or city marker. |

Run `npm.cmd --prefix data run consolidate` from the repository root to regenerate the CSV. It uses the published municipal weather release, original historical exports, and the JSON generated by `prepare-field-references.py`. The earlier six-site UTC verification values are included through the municipal release. The generator checks that the overlapping values agree and rejects conflicting values for a duplicate key. Locations that share satellite grid readings keep their own rows and grid IDs.

Original flat CSVs and superseded extracts are kept as compressed copies under `raw/csv-archive/` or as `.csv.gz` beside their verification data. The synthetic CSVs in `tests/fixtures/` exercise the importer.

## Crop rotation and rainfall inputs

The website uses records from 2015 through 2024. Original download queries are listed in [References.txt](References.txt).

| Input | Coverage and use |
| --- | --- |
| Archived `Daily` exports | Six NASA POWER points, one per Bicol province. Daily rainfall in mm/day and Local Solar Time. |
| Archived `Monthly` exports and `Monthly/Power_libmanan.json` | Monthly rainfall, mean/minimum/maximum temperature, and relative humidity at seven POWER points, including Libmanan. Annual summaries are included in the consolidated CSV. |
| Archived `Imerg` export | Monthly GPM IMERG Final Run V07 rainfall averaged over the data bounding box, 122.35–124.45° E and 11.95–14.15° N. The chart uses the area average. |

Run `npm.cmd --prefix data run generate:climate` from the repository root after installing the data package. The command reads the original exports, including compressed CSVs and the Libmanan JSON, and produces [the website climate bundle](../web/src/data/historical-climate.json).

The publisher converts monthly POWER rates from mm/day to monthly depths using the actual number of calendar days, including leap days. It converts IMERG’s monthly mean rate from mm/hour using the actual number of hours in each month. It preserves zero rainfall and represents missing values as `null`. Seasonal rainfall is the mean monthly depth for the same calendar month across the available years. Legazpi and Sorsogon have identical daily and monthly values in the supplied POWER files. Naga and Libmanan have identical monthly values. [NASA POWER](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_meteorology/) · [NASA IMERG V07](https://gpm.nasa.gov/resources/documents/imerg-v07-technical-documentation)

Each place uses its named climate point when available, or the supplied reference point for its province. A sampled field point uses the nearest supplied climate point. The datasets provide historical climate context. Field-specific advice also requires local soil, drainage, water availability, crop history, and locally reviewed rules. [DA–BSWM soil series](https://www.bswm.da.gov.ph/download/philippine-soil-series/)

## 2024 municipal weather

**2024 daily weather** covers Bicol’s 107 municipalities and seven cities. Each metric uses the source grid cell nearest the selected place’s mapped town or city coordinates. Towns in the same cell share its estimate. The website displays the cell center and native resolution. Each place has all 366 UTC days available for all 13 measures. [NASA POWER daily API](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/daily/) · [NASA POWER hydrology](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_hydrology/)

| Source | Daily measures | Resolution | Cells used |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| MERRA-2 | Corrected rainfall, mean/minimum/maximum temperature, dew point, relative humidity, 2 m wind speed, land evaporation, and transpiration energy | 0.5° latitude × 0.625° longitude | 17 |
| CERES SYN1deg | Solar radiation | 1° × 1° | 10 |
| GPM IMERG | Rainfall | 0.1° × 0.1° | 91 |
| SMAP Level 4 | Surface and root-zone soil moisture | Approximately 9 km | 92 |

Run `npm.cmd --prefix data run fetch:municipal-power` to assemble the dataset. The script reuses the original responses in `data/raw/power-bicol-2024/`. It retrieves the standard weather grids for the full year, IMERG in monthly regional batches, and SMAP at the selected cell centers. The [place-to-cell manifest](../web/src/data/power-municipal-2024/manifest.json) records the assignments. The [source manifest](../web/src/data/power-municipal-2024/sources.json) records each request and API version. The [SMAP coordinate subset](smap-bicol-grid.json) comes from the NASA POWER daily UTC archive.

NASA POWER supplies `EVLAND` as land evaporation in mm/day and `EVPTRNS` as the energy flux from plant transpiration in MJ/m²/day. FieldWise uses the returned units and labels. Minimum temperature, dew point, and wind at 2 m provide inputs for an FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration estimate alongside the existing weather data. [NASA MERRA-2 file specification](https://gmao.gsfc.nasa.gov/media/gmaoftp/mikeb/Harvey/MERRA2.README.pdf) · [FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration](https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e08.htm)

## 2024 six-site NASA POWER verification

The six-site verification dataset contains a full UTC year of corrected precipitation, mean and maximum air temperature at 2 meters, relative humidity at 2 meters, and all-sky surface solar radiation. The [release manifest](../web/src/data/power-2024/manifest.json) records the request URLs, retrieval time, units, and coverage. Each site has 366 days for each measure. [Referencing NASA POWER](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/referencing/)

The [site register](sites.json) uses the six points on the FieldWise map in Legazpi, Daet, Naga, Virac, Masbate City, and Sorsogon City. Each point has a positive elevation in the bundled Mapzen place samples and falls within its geographic province in the 2020 geoBoundaries ADM2 polygons from NAMRIA, PSA, and OCHA Philippines. Legazpi and Sorsogon share a MERRA-2 cell. The readings describe gridded estimates at the requested points. [Philippine ADM2 source](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/PHL/ADM2/) · [Mapzen source credits](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md)

The public API requires no key. From the repository root, run these commands to save the original six-site CSV responses as `.csv.gz` in `data/raw/`, normalize them, and retrieve the matching IMERG and SMAP data.

```powershell
npm.cmd --prefix data install
npm.cmd --prefix data run fetch:power
npm.cmd --prefix data run fetch:power -- --hydrology
```

The hydrology command saves verified JSON downloads and a manifest under `data/raw/power-hydrology-2024/`. It requests IMERG precipitation (0.1°) and SMAP surface and root-zone soil moisture (about 9 km) independently for the same sites and UTC dates. Each site has 366 days for each measure. The API's CSV headers identified the high-resolution parameters as MERRA-2 during the sample check. The high-resolution JSON responses explicitly reported `IMERG` or `SMAP` in `header.sources`, which the downloader checks. [NASA POWER hydrology](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_hydrology/)

An October rainfall spot check found the same peak date at Legazpi in the POWER grid and the PAGASA station record, October 22, 2024. POWER reported 205.96 mm for its UTC day and PAGASA recorded 431.0 mm at the station. The station report gives the calendar date, so the daily accumulation windows could not be confirmed as equivalent. A full daily station comparison requires the matching observations and reporting windows. [PAGASA Legazpi station extremes](https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/cad/CLIMATOLOGICAL%20EXTREMES%20%28as%20of%202024%29/Legazpi.pdf) · [PAGASA climate data](https://origin.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/climate/climate-data)

## NASA POWER importer

To import one registered site's daily CSV, create the output directory if needed and run the importer.

```powershell
New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force -Path data/processed
node data/import-power.mjs --site albay-01 data/raw/albay-01-2024.csv.gz data/processed/albay-01-2024.json
```

The importer validates site metadata, dates, numeric readings, duplicates, and usable coverage before replacing an existing output. It keeps the source header and normalizes the five weather measures to the FieldWise schema.

## Soil and climate references for field planning

The original downloads are in `data/raw/field-planning/`. The extracts are included in the consolidated CSV.

| Download | Extract and coverage |
| --- | --- |
| [DA–BSWM Philippine Soil Series](https://www.bswm.da.gov.ph/download/philippine-soil-series/) | The national archive contains 539 records with soil series, map-unit descriptions, agroecological zone codes, and hectares. The download page lists an April 24, 2026 update. The `bswm-soilgrids` rows match 105 of 114 markers to source polygons using the Luzon 1911 coordinate system and EPSG transformation 1161. |
| [ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0](https://docs.isric.org/globaldata/soilgrids/SoilGrids_faqs_01.html) | Five Bicol raster subsets provide mean predictions for pH, organic carbon, sand, silt, and clay at 5–15 cm depth on the native 250 m grid. The soil CSV has all five predictions at 57 markers. Masked cells are blank in the CSV. ISRIC excludes areas such as urban land and inland water through its soil mask. The local `soilgrids-downloads.json` records the exact coverage requests. |
| [DOST–PAGASA station normals](https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/cad/CLIMATOLOGICAL%20NORMALS%20%281991-2020%29/) | The `pagasa-station-normals` rows hold 48 monthly records for Daet, Legazpi, Masbate, and Virac Synop, including rainfall, rainy days, temperature, humidity, and vapor pressure. Use matching 1991–2020 periods for comparisons with modeled climatologies. |
| [PhilRice Bicol research report](https://www.philrice.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2021-Highlights-BS-Bicol.pdf#page=10) | PRiSM estimated that Bicol rice planting peaked in January and July in 2021. Its 2021 Bicol first-semester validation sample was too small to check map accuracy. The observations describe regional planting patterns in that year. |

The soil CSV samples the existing city and town markers. A field or cropland location can have different properties. The SoilGrids columns are modeled estimates and the BSWM columns describe intersecting map units. Field measurements remain the basis for field-specific soil and fertilizer decisions. [DA–BSWM FertMap](https://nshp.bswm.da.gov.ph/fertmap/) · [ISRIC SoilGrids documentation](https://docs.isric.org/globaldata/soilgrids/index.html)

To rebuild the soil and station records after downloading the source files, use Python with `pypdf`, `pyshp`, `pyproj`, `shapely`, and `rasterio` installed. Run `python data/prepare-field-references.py` and then `npm.cmd --prefix data run consolidate` from the repository root.
